I

Know Your Career

Across three drives in September 2025, December 2025 and May 2026, thirty mentor organisations hosted Grade 9 and Grade 10 students for 500 one-day internships spanning more than nineteen sectors. Participating organisations included Dhruva Space, MosChip Semiconductor, Deloitte, Samvad Partners, Aragen Life Sciences, Fourth Partner Energy, Darwinbox, T-Hub, Care Hospitals, Arete Hospitals, Caspian Investments, Continental Coffee and Jayanti Reddy Designs.

Three role-model sessions were conducted for Grades 9 and 10, at which twenty-five speakers addressed a total of seven hundred students. Fields covered ranged from medicine, law and chartered accountancy to aviation, journalism, product design, wildlife photography and artificial intelligence.

A one-week project internship was piloted for Grades 11 and 12 with two mentor organisations, Century Mattresses and Samvad Partners. Twenty students completed the project. The pilot is now under review for expansion.

II New Initiative

AI Readiness

In view of the pace at which artificial intelligence is reshaping education and employment, the Governance Board and school leadership approved the formation of a task force on AI readiness for students and teachers. It brings together the school leadership, faculty, the Innovation Lab and external experts.

A sum of ₹50 lakh is being raised to fund AI exposure and hands-on building for students. In-principle approval for a CSR contribution has been received from one large corporate.

The first leadership talk in the series, “The Human Edge”, was held in Bangalore on 18 April 2026. Panellists were Mr. T. K. Kurien ’75, CEO of Premji Invest; Prof. Anil Ananthaswamy of IIT Madras, author of Why Machines Learn; Mr. Mohan Narayanaswamy ’97; and Ms. Nidhi Reddy ’92 of the Management Committee. The next talk is expected on the Hyderabad campus.

 

III

Governance and Funds

A Governance Board was constituted, bringing together the school leadership, the Society’s Management Committee and the programme’s founding sponsor groups. The Board has met three times since 2024 and meets two to three times a year under a defined charter.

Contributions to date stand at ₹43.18 lakh against an endowment target of ₹10 crore, sized to sustain the programme in perpetuity. Interest of ₹1,66,660 accrued to the endowment has financed the salary of the programme’s Junior Programme Executive on an ongoing basis. Marketing, technology and community functions, including a custom-built CRM and the programme website, are delivered by alumni volunteers without a paid team.
